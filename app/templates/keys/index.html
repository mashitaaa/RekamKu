Where each key lives

Account key (UMK)
Random 256-bit key, wrapped by a key derived from your password (scrypt + server pepper) and by your recovery code.
File keys
One random key per file, wrapped by your account key.
Share keys
One random 256-bit key per approved request. Stored only wrapped: by the patient's account key and, after the key is added, by the requester's account key. File keys you share are wrapped by it.
Your RSA private key
Stored encrypted with your account key. It is used only to open share keys sent to you.
Your signing key
A separate RSA key pair, also stored encrypted with your account key. It is used only to sign PDFs, never to decrypt.
