How it works

Three steps from a messy phone gallery to records you can show any doctor.

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Your password creates the key to your vault. You also get a recovery code, so a forgotten password never means lost records.

  2. 2

    Add your records

    Upload a KTP scan, a lab PDF or a seizure video. Each file is encrypted while it uploads, with its own key.

  3. 3

    Open them at your check-up

    Sign in on any device, open or download the file, and show it to your doctor. Videos play straight away.

Three layers of encryption, one rule: if it changed, you never see it.

Every file is split into 1 MiB pieces. Each piece goes through DES, then RC4, then AES-256-GCM, with keys unique to that file. When you open it, every piece is checked first. A single flipped bit and the file is refused.
