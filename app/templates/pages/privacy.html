Edit your health profile; upload a file again to replace it.

An activity log without file names or content, under Account & security.

{{ icon('lock') }}Who can open your data

Only you. Each file key is wrapped by your account key, and your account key is wrapped by a key derived from your password (scrypt) and a server secret. Someone holding both the database and the server secret still cannot open your vault without your password or recovery code. The flip side: if you lose both, nobody can recover your data.
